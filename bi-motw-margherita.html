The Story

Garganelli drops for about three minutes. It is fresh, so pull it before it looks done — it keeps cooking in the pan. Mushrooms and braised beef hit the hot pan first, then the wine goes in and has to burn off. Raw wine in the pan tastes raw on the plate.

The finish makes it. Compound butter, parmesan, chives, and sour cream go in together with a little pasta water, and it gets tossed until it reads as one sauce instead of butter sitting on noodles.

Maccheroni al Pettine · Romagna
Garganelli comes from Romagna, where it is also called maccheroni al pettine — comb macaroni. Each square of egg dough is rolled around a stick and pressed over a ridged comb. Those ridges are why it holds a sauce this heavy.
