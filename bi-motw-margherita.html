Bucatini Bolognese
Fresh Bucatini · House Meat Sauce · Made In-House
The Story

The hole is the shape. Bucatini is pushed through a die that leaves a channel running the length of every strand, so sauce rides inside the noodle as well as outside. One strand carries more than a solid one does. Toss it in the pan — never ladle sauce over the top and send it.

Fresh changes the clock. It cooks in a fraction of the time dry does and it keeps cooking after it leaves the water, so pull it early and finish it in the sauce with a little pasta water.

The Name · Rome
Bucatini comes from buco — Italian for hole. It is a Roman shape, extruded through a die rather than rolled and cut. Handle the raw strands gently: squeeze one and the hole closes before it ever hits the water.
