Branzino Via Carota
Open Faced · Lemon Butter · Capers · Broccolini
The Story

Season both sides with the salt and pepper mix, then an ounce of blended oil goes on the flat top. The fish lays flesh side down and cooks through in about five minutes. Flesh side down is where the color comes from.

Sauce it in order — lemon butter first, via carota sauce over the top. Finish with chopped parsley, capers, and a lemon cheek. It plates open faced on the large oval, so a sloppy sauce line shows from across the room.

Via Carota · West Village
Via Carota is an Italian restaurant in New York's West Village, opened in November 2014 by Jody Williams and Rita Sodi. They named it after a street in the Italian town where Sodi used to live. On our line the name means one thing: open faced, lemon butter, and capers.
