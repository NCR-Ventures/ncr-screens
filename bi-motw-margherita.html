Pesto Genovese
Fresh pipe rigate · Pesto sauce · Zucchini · Parmesan
The Story

The sauce is batched, the plate is not. Pesto sauce is made ahead in the Vitamix: basil, parsley, pine nuts, parmesan, garlic, and oil. It goes on the hot pasta off the flame with a splash of pasta water so it coats instead of clumps. Pesto that sits in a hot pan turns brown and bitter.

The pasta does the work. Fresh pipe rigate is ridged and hollow, so the pesto gets inside every piece. Zucchini and sun-dried tomatoes go in last for bite and color. Grilled chicken is an add, not a default. Ask.

Where It Comes From
Genoa, in Liguria. “Pesto” comes from pestare, to crush — the original was pounded by hand in a marble mortar. Genovese means it is the basil version, the one every other pesto is named after.
