BASTILLE DAY
France's National Holiday — Vive la France!
JULY 14
01
The Storming
Parisians stormed the Bastille prison on July 14, 1789 — the spark that lit the French Revolution.
02
Not Its Real Name
France doesn't call it "Bastille Day." Officially, it's La Fête Nationale — the National Day.
03
Made Official
France made July 14 an official holiday in 1880, honoring the storming and the unity that followed it.
04
How Paris Celebrates
Paris marks it with a military parade down the Champs-Élysées and fireworks over the Eiffel Tower.
05
The Motto It Gave Us
"Liberté, Égalité, Fraternité" — the rallying cry born from this era is still France's motto today.
