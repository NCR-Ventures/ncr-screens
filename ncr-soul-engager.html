NCR Ventures 3 Opportunities to Be Better Week Ending 6.14 — Patios Full, Servers on a First-Name Basis
5 of 5
1
Fresh Bread, Every Basket
Bread is the first bite of the meal and the easiest to get right. A couple of tables met stale or hard bread. Check the basket before it leaves the kitchen.
2
Temp and Taste at the Pass
Proteins were the soft spot again — a soggy pizza, a dry main, a flat steak. The fix is at the line: check temp, check seasoning, and don't send a plate you wouldn't eat.
3
Clear on the Guest's Cue
A plate got cleared with food still on it, and a dessert got pushed after a guest said no. Read the table. Ask before you reach. The guest sets the pace.
