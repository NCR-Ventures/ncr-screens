NCR Ventures 3 Opportunities to Be Better Week Ending 5.24 — Graduation Season Has Arrived
5 of 5
1
"We Were Told to Hurry Up" — Three Weeks Running
A guest flew in from Atlanta to meet a friend's fiancée. One drink in, she was told to hurry up because a graduation party had reservations in 45 minutes. She posted the same one-star review to Google, Yelp, and TripAdvisor. No table turn is worth that. If a reservation is coming and we're behind, the move is to comp a course, not to rush the guest. Never. Tell. A. Guest. To. Hurry.
2
Birthday Flags Are Slipping — Now Four Weeks Straight
A guest noted a birthday on the reservation. Server didn't catch it. No dessert, no acknowledgment. Guest wrote: "Why ask if celebrating an occasion when making reservation to not acknowledge while there?" That question is the entire problem. Pre-shift: read every res-note out loud. Assign one person to own the occasion. Every flag gets touched. Every shift.
3
"My Booth Was Promised. I Got the Bar Instead."
A guest called ahead, requested a specific booth for a birthday, was told yes. Got sat in the bar. Too loud. Debated leaving before the server even arrived. The promise on the phone is a contract. If we can't keep it, we call the guest before they drive over — or we make the seat right when they arrive. The host stand is where five-star nights start or die.
