Black Angus Tenderloin · USDA Prime · House Cut to Order
The Story

Black Angus tenderloin, butchered in-house and cut to order. Seasoned, seared, and char-grilled to lock in juices and build a flavorful crust. Served à la carte with our signature onion straws and a roasted romano tomato.

Filet comes from the tenderloin — a non-working muscle on the cow, which is what gives it that soft, buttery texture. The most tender cut on the menu, and the reason it carries the price tag it does.

Recommend medium rare — a warm red center, 130-134°F, beginning to firm up with red juices. The doneness that lets the cut speak for itself.
