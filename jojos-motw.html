Classic Eggs Benedict
English muffin · Canadian bacon · Poached eggs · Hollandaise · Asparagus · Potatoes peperonata
The Story

Built from the muffin up. House-made English muffin, split and griddled. Thick-cut Canadian bacon seared so the edges char. Two poached eggs, whites set and yolks still running. Hollandaise spooned over at the window, never ahead of time.

This one is growing brunch. Shaved asparagus and potatoes peperonata on the side instead of plain hash. It is one of the stars of the JoJo’s brunch menu and a big reason that business keeps climbing. Know it, sell it, and send it out looking like the photo every time.

Where It Comes From
Two New York restaurants still argue over it. Delmonico’s says 1860, in its own ovens. The Waldorf says 1894, when stockbroker Lemuel Benedict ordered poached eggs, bacon, toast, and hollandaise to fix a hangover. Either way, the hollandaise makes or breaks it. Broken hollandaise does not leave the kitchen.
