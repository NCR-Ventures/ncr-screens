Spicy Vodka Rigatoni
Fresh Rigatoni · Vodka Sauce · Chili Butter · Parmesan · Pecorino Snow
The Story

Six minutes. Not seven. The rigatoni is fresh, so it comes out of the water before it looks ready. Sauce, heavy cream, chili butter, and butter all go in together and get tossed as one thing, not layered. Parmesan last, then pecorino snow over the top in the bowl.

The sauce is the whole dish. Those onions sweat in butter on low heat for thirty to forty minutes and never take on color. Rush them or let them brown and you have a different sauce. Vodka, tomatoes, and Calabrian peppers go in after, cream at the very end.

Carbone Style · Thompson Street
"Carbone Style" points at Carbone, the Italian-American restaurant on Thompson Street in New York founded by Mario Carbone and Rich Torrisi. On our line the style comes down to the onions: half an hour in butter, no color.
