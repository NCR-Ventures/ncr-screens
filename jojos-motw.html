The Story

Two sauces on purpose. The St. Elmo's is horseradish-forward and sharp enough to clear your head. The rémoulade is the opposite move — cool, creamy, a little tangy. Some guests will go back and forth all night. Nobody should have to ask for the second one.

Everything here is cold or it is nothing. Shrimp chilled through, ice fresh and crushed, the glass cold before it is built. A shrimp cocktail that arrives cool instead of cold is the fastest complaint on the menu.

The Sauce · Indianapolis, 1902
St. Elmo Steak House has served the same shrimp cocktail since it opened in 1902 — for most of its history the only appetizer on the menu. The sauce is built on fresh-ground horseradish, and the kitchen goes through tons of it a year. Servers there warn guests not to breathe in over the first bite.
