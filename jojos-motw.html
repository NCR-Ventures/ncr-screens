Vodka Rigatoni
Calabrian Chilies · Jammy Onions · Tomato Cream
The Story

Rigatoni dressed in a spicy vodka cream — slow-cooked sweet onions, Calabrian chili paste, premium tomato, heavy cream. Emulsified to a glossy sauce that clings to every ridge.

The work is in the onions. Cooked low and slow in butter and water — never browned, never rushed. Hours, not minutes. They turn jammy and sweet, and that is the backbone of the sauce.

Calabrian chilies are not red pepper flakes. Fermented and jarred — sweet, smoky, complex heat rather than a sharp burn. Finish the pan with butter and pasta water to lock it together.
