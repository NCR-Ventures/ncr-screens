Aqua Pazza Scampi
Shrimp · Charred Tomato · Calabrian Peppers · Reggiano · Crostini
The Story

Shrimp poached in a spicy aqua pazza broth — charred tomato, Calabrian peppers, garlic, white wine. Finished with grated Reggiano, served with rustic crostini.

Aqua pazza — "crazy water" — is a Neapolitan fisherman's dish. Fish cooked in seawater with tomato, oil, garlic, and chili. The broth carries everything; the bread mops it up.

Scampi traditionally is a butter-wine-garlic preparation. Here the butter steps aside for the aqua pazza broth — lighter, brighter, with the Calabrian heat.
