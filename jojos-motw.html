Ricotta Gnocchi
English Peas · Charred Mushrooms · Roasted Garlic Pesto
The Story

Hand-rolled ricotta gnocchi — light as they should be, pillowy and tender, never heavy. Tossed in a roasted garlic pesto built on fresh basil and parsley.

Finished with sweet spring English peas and earthy charred mushrooms. A clean, vegetable-forward plate that lets the gnocchi do the work.

Chef's Note · Landon Barnes
The pesto leans on roasted garlic instead of raw — sweeter, mellower, more depth. Pairs with the ricotta without fighting it.
