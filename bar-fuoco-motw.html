Pizzette Regina
Small Pizza · Neapolitan · Wood-fired
The Story

Regina means the queen — and this one earns it. The Margherita is the original Neapolitan pizza, created in 1889 for Queen Margherita of Savoy. Three ingredients, no shortcuts: San Marzano tomato, fresh mozzarella, basil. The fire does the rest.

At Fuoco we run it as a pizzette — smaller, crispier, built for the bar. The dough blisters at the edge, the cheese pools in the center, and the basil goes on after the fire. Classic technique. No substitutions. The queen doesn't negotiate.

The Spark
The original Margherita was made in Naples in 1889 by pizzaiolo Raffaele Esposito — the colors of the pizza matched the Italian flag. Tomato red, mozzarella white, basil green. Some things don't need improvement.
