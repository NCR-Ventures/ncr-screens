Roman Artichokes
Grilled · Basil Pesto · Pine Nut · Parmesan · Arugula
The Story

Grilled Roman artichokes set over a basil pesto with pine nuts, olive oil, and parmesan. Plated with fresh arugula.

The Carciofo Romanesco is the artichoke of Rome — large, purple-tipped, heart-heavy. Roman cooking elevated it to an art form. The char carries the flavor.

Pesto Genovese: crushed basil, pine nuts, garlic, olive oil, parmesan. The word "pesto" means crushed — the technique older than the recipe. Bright green, sharp, cuts the artichoke's earthiness.

Carciofo Romanesco · The Roman Artichoke
