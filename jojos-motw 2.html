JoJo's "Waldorf"
Endive · Celery · Frisée · Bibb · Apricot · Pistachio Vinaigrette
The Story

A reimagined Waldorf — crisp endive, celery, frisée, and tender bibb tossed with apricot and pistachio in a bright pistachio vinaigrette.

A touch of funky blue cheese ties it all together. Familiar at first read, then it surprises you.

Blue Cheese
Chef's Note · Landon Barnes
The blue cheese is meant to be funky, not subtle — that's the point. Plays off the sweet apricot and toasted pistachio. Toss to coat, don't drown it.
