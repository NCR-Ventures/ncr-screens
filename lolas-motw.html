Chicken Cordon Bleu
Jambon · Gruyère · Mustard Crème · Chervil
The Story

Jambon and gruyère stuffed inside a hand-pounded, hand-breaded chicken breast. Pan-fried crispy, set over a mustard crème sauce, finished with chervil.

A French classic, done the long way. Each piece is pounded, stuffed, breaded, and cooked to order — every step by hand. There is no shortcut version of this dish on the line.

Orwell worked the kitchens at a Paris hotel in 1929. He called the cook who got it done no matter what a débrouillard — the one who finds a way. This is that cook's dish.
