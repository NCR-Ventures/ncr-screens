The Story

The glaze is the whole pick-up. Portion into a black steel pan, four minutes in the oven. The veg set simmers in braising liquid, chicken stock, shallot, and cold butter, then goes back in. Baste the top and glaze it under the broiler until it is shiny and hot all the way through.

Plate it the same way every time. Chicken sits bottom left of the bowl and gets flooded with the rest of the sauce. Veg set on the side, never piled on top. Frisee, watercress, and fine herbs dressed in olive oil go on last, right before it leaves.

Coq Means Rooster
Coq is French for rooster. The name comes from braising a tough old bird long enough to turn it tender, though almost everyone makes it with chicken now. Red Burgundy is the usual wine, but French regions have always braised it in whatever they drink — Riesling in Alsace, vin jaune in the Jura.
