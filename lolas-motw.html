Asparagus · Mustard Greens · Cherry Tomato-Caper Sauce Vierge
The Story

Pan-seared Catalina bass with crispy skin. Plated over asparagus and petite mustard greens, finished with a cherry tomato-caper sauce vierge.

À la provençale — the southern French Mediterranean treatment. Olive oil, tomato, garlic, herbs from the sun-baked Provence hillsides. Light, bright, built around what grows on the coast.

Sauce vierge — "virgin sauce" — was created by Michel Guérard in 1976, one of the founders of nouvelle cuisine. The ingredients stay raw — never "deflowered" by cooking. Pure tomato, pure herb, pure oil.
