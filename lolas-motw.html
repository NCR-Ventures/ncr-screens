The Story

Charred leeks over sauce gribiche. Toasted hazelnuts for crunch, fresh chervil to finish. Simple, sharp, structured.

Sauce gribiche is a cold French classic — hard-boiled egg yolks emulsified with oil, mustard, and vinegar, finished with capers, cornichons, and herbs. A bistro standby that earns its place on the plate.

The char sweetens the leek and adds bitterness at the edges. Gribiche brings acid and herb. Hazelnut closes the loop. A vegetable dish that eats like a main.
