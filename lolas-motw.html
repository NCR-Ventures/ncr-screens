Prawn Cocktail
Four Jumbo Shrimp · Chilled Through · Built to Order
The Story

Four shrimp, ice, sauce, lemon. There is nowhere to hide on a plate this simple — it is cold or it is wrong. Chill the coupe, pack it with fresh crushed ice, and build it the moment it is ordered. Ice that has been sitting turns to water and the whole thing goes slack.

Hang the shrimp clean over the rim so all four read at once, and keep the tails pointing the same way. This is the first plate a lot of tables see. It sets what they expect from everything after it.

Prawn or Shrimp · The Real Answer
There is no culinary difference — the two words get used interchangeably, and "prawn" is simply what most of the English-speaking world says. Biologists do separate them, but not by size, and not in a way any kitchen menu has ever followed.
