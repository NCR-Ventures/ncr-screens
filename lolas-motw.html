Beef Stroganoff
Seared beef · Mushrooms · Egg noodles · Sour cream
The Story

Sear first, sauce second. The beef gets a hard sear and comes out before the sauce starts, so it stays tender instead of boiling in the pan. Mushrooms go in next and take on the fond, then the sauce builds on top of that.

Comfort food with a backbone. Wide noodles catch the sauce, the sour cream goes on at the end so it stays cool and bright against the warm gravy, and parsley finishes it. It should taste rich, not heavy.

Where It Comes From
Russian, named for the Stroganov family. The first printed recipe is from 1871, in Elena Molokhovets’ A Gift to Young Housewives — no mushrooms, no noodles. Those came later, and the noodles are an American habit.
