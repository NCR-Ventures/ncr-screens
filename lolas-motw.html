Onion Soup Gratinée
Caramelized Onions · Beef Stock · House Cheese Mix · Gratinée
The Story

Caramelized onions, beef stock, our house cheese mix gratinéed to a golden crust over a slice of bread. Served in a classic ceramic crock, finished with chives.

Onion soup traces to the Les Halles markets in 19th-century Paris, where butchers and porters ate it before dawn after the night shift. From the markets to the bistros — and to every French menu since.

The work is in the onions. Long, slow caramelization, then beef stock and a long simmer. Bread, cheese mix, under the broiler until it bubbles and crisps.
