SOP of the Week
The White Shirt
FOH Appearance Standard
SOP-FOH-DRESS-001
Every Shift · Every Server
White. Pressed. Sharp.
The guest reads you before they order — before you speak, before the water hits the glass. Give them nothing to doubt.
The Standard
A crisp white shirt is not a shirt — it is the room’s first sentence. Everything the guest will think about tonight starts here.
01
Pressed.
The dry cleaner is the standard. If you can’t iron it that sharp, take it to the cleaner. No wrinkles, no fold lines, no “it’ll relax.”
02
Clean.
White means white. No stains, no collar ring, no pit marks. A white tee underneath keeps it sharp.
03
Sharp.
Fully buttoned, tucked in, belt on. Sleeves down and buttoned — or cleanly rolled and matching, not random.
04
Backup.
A second pressed shirt in your locker. Spills happen mid-shift — you change, you don’t finish dirty.
You are the first thing the guest sees. Walk the floor looking like you mean it.
