The 2-Minute Checkback
Return within two minutes — or two bites — of dropping the food. Every table, every time.
Confirm it's right — don't just ask if it's fine. A specific question catches a problem while it's still fixable.
✓
Ask This
"How is the steak cooked for you?"
"Does everyone have their sauces and sides?"
"Can I refresh anyone's drink?"
✕
Not This
"Is everything okay?"
"How's everything tasting?"
"Good so far?"
Why It Matters
Unhappy guests rarely complain — they just don't come back. Catching it in the first two minutes is the difference between a fix and a lost guest.
