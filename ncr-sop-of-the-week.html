SOP-044  ·  Effective 11.1.26
The Pre-Shift
Two a day, minimum. Ten to fifteen minutes. Standing up. Phones away. A salaried manager leads it, and the Chef presents the food — every time.
0:00–1:00
Line-Up
Full uniform, phones away. Walk the line: appearance, pen, wine key, crumber, lighter, attitude.
1:00–4:00
The Board
Covers, pacing, large parties, VIPs by name, allergies, 86s, push items, features with the price.
4:00–8:00
Teach One Thing
Chef presents the feature. Taste it. Two people repeat it back as if to a guest.
8:00–11:00
The Hospitality Moment
One real guest review read aloud. The focus for the week. A Soul Engager shout-out.
11:00–13:00
Numbers and the Game
Yesterday vs. same day last year. Where we are for the week. The contest, with a prize worth winning.
13:00–15:00
Open the Floor and Close
Sixty seconds for questions. Then ask three people: "What is the one thing tonight?"
THE ONE THING.
Every pre-shift ends with one thing. Not four. One. Nobody takes a table without a pre-shift, and it never ends on a negative.

SOP-044  ·  The Weekly Rhythm
Meetings and Calls
Five meetings. Five owners. One flow. Off on meeting day means call in or Zoom — the meeting does not move.
Twice daily
10–15 min
Pre-Shift
Before lunch and before dinner. All FOH on shift, Chef presents the food. Led by the manager on duty.
Weekly
60–90 min
Store L-10 Manager Meeting
Same day, same time, same place. Every salaried manager and chef in the building. Led by the MP / GM.
Monday 8:30 AM
30–60 min
Monday Focus Call
All MPs / GMs and Executive Chefs, company-wide. Sets the focus for the week. Led by the Home Office facilitator.
Every other Wed
30–60 min
GM Call
All MPs / GMs. COGS, EBITDA, housekeeping notes, one best practice. Led by a GM, on rotation.
Every other week
30–60 min
Chef Call — by Brand
Executive Chefs by brand. Food cost, recipe adherence, rollouts, kitchen people. Led by the Corporate Chef.
THE FLOW.
Monday Focus Call → GM Call and Chef Call → Store L-10 → Pre-Shift → The Guest. If it doesn't reach the server at table 12, the meeting didn't happen.
