SOP-040
Allergen Awareness
A guest with a food allergy is trusting a stranger with their airway.
Governing Principle
Nobody guesses. Not the server, not the bartender, not the sous chef on a Saturday night. "I think that's fine" is not an answer anyone at NCR Ventures is authorized to give.

SOP of the Week
When a Guest Declares an Allergy
Six steps, every time, regardless of how minor the guest makes it sound.
1
Stop and get a chef
The server does not recommend, reassure, or rule anything out. The chef comes to the table.
2
Chef confirms the details at the table
The specific allergen, and whether it is an allergy, an intolerance, or a preference. "Shellfish" is not specific enough.
3
Chef checks the recipe and the label
Built from the allergen matrix and the physical label in hand — not from memory. Labels change without notice.
4
Flag the ticket
Allergen entered in the POS so it prints on every copy. Verbal-only to the line is a violation.
5
Build it with the allergy kit
Clean hands, fresh gloves, sealed kit, fresh product from a sealed container. Never on line equipment.
6
Chef delivers it
Directly to the guest, stated out loud as the allergy plate. Never a runner, never the general run.
REACTION? CALL 911 FIRST.
Every time. Do not wait to see if it gets worse. Keep the plate, the ticket, and the packaging. Do not clear the table.
