SOP of the Week  ·  Table Resets
Spray the Towel, Not the Table
Sanitizer goes on the cloth in your hand. It never goes into the air of a dining room.
Why
Spray the table and the overspray lands on the chairs, on the guests next to it, and on the food at the table beside it. Nobody ordered sanitizer. Spray the towel and the only thing that gets wet is the table you're wiping.
Every Reset, Every Time
1
Clear it first
Everything off the table and into the bus tub. Crumbs into the tub or your hand — never onto a chair or the floor.
2
Spray the towel, low and away
Hold the towel below table height, pointed away from guests, and spray the cloth. Two or three pumps is plenty.
3
Wipe the whole table
Top, edges, and the seats. One pass, no streaks.
4
Reset and go
Push the chairs in square, set the table, and get the bus tub out of sight before the next guest is seated.
NEVER NEAR A GUEST.
Guests at the next table, food on the pass, a server carrying plates — if anyone is within arm's reach, wait. Then spray the towel.

Know the Menu  ·  Why It's Called That
Why Our Martini Is the Paul "A"
Every guest who asks deserves the real answer. Here it is.

Our classic vodka martini is named for Paul Granzier — Pat Granzier's father. Pat wanted to honor him, and this is how.

Paul was an old-school guy. Pat remembers his father walking through the door after work every night and relaxing with one classic vodka martini. Not two. One. Every night. It was a ritual.

Our service is a ritual too — the same care, the same way, every table, every night. That's why his name is on the glass.

"Paul was a consummate gentleman who loved a good book, a good pun, a good cocktail, and was forever proud of his wife and seven sons."
The Granzier family
ONE DRINK. ONE RITUAL.
When a guest asks about the Paul "A," tell them the story. It takes twenty seconds and it's the best thing on the menu.
