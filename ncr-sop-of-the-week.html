Station Reference Card
Steak Standard
Know Your Steaks. Know Your Temps.
Seasoned · Seared · Char-Grilled
The Cuts
Filet
Most tender. Non-working muscle — soft, buttery texture.
Ribeye
Superior marbling — the most flavor.
Strip
The heartiest cut. Bold, beefy bite.
Porterhouse
Tenderloin and strip in one cut — best of both.
Sirloin
The leanest — therefore more dense.
The Temps
Rare
120–129°F
Cool, bright red center. Soft to the touch.
Medium Rare
130–134°F
Warm red center. Beginning to firm with red juices.
Medium
135–144°F
Warm pink center. Outer portions browning, red juices.
Medium Well
145–154°F
Slightly pink center. Firm to the touch, brown juices.
Well Done
155–164°F
No pink or red. Firm to the touch throughout.
Ground Beef
160°F
Fully brown throughout. No pink — food safety minimum.
Our steaks are seasoned, seared, and char-grilled to lock in juices and build a flavorful crust.
