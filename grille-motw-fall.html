Pan Roasted Half Chicken
Whipped potatoes · Natural au jus · Limited availability
The Story

Pan roasted, not oven-and-forget. The half chicken starts skin-down in the pan so the skin crisps, then finishes roasting until the meat pulls clean from the bone. Rosemary, garlic, and parsley go on at the end so they taste fresh, not cooked out.

Limited availability. When we’re out, we’re out. Breast sliced and fanned, thigh and leg stacked on whipped potatoes, natural au jus pooled underneath. Never prep too many. The goal is to 86 it between 8:30 and 9:00 pm, not to have a pan of them left at close.

Chef’s Note
Au jus is not gravy. It is the roasting drippings, skimmed and reduced — no roux, no thickener. If it coats the spoon, it went too far.
