Check it. Seal it. Photograph it.

The handoff standard, every bag, every shift
1
Check the ticket against the bag item by item: sides, sauces, dressings, desserts, and pizza.
2
Mark every separate piece “1 of 2,” “2 of 2,” and so on.
3
Seal every bag. Staple the ticket and add a sticker with the order number.
4
Confirm the guest’s name and order number with the Dasher or guest. Say the piece count out loud.
5
Photograph the sealed bag before it leaves. No photo, no winning a wrong-order dispute.
6
Chefs: pull delivery steaks a temp early and vent the box, fresh fryer oil, and nothing on the delivery menu that doesn’t travel.
