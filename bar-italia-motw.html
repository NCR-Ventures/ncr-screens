Pizza Dough
The standard · Same-day dough · Room temp proof
5 hrs
Room temp before use. Hard number, not a range
Label
Time made and time it can be used. Every tray
Never
In the cooler. Room temperature only

Same-day dough sits out five hours. Portioned balls go on sheet trays, get wrapped, get labeled, and sit at room temperature for five hours before anyone stretches one. It used to be four to six. It is five now. Not four and a half because the rail is backed up.

Read the label before you pull a tray. Time made on the left, time it can be used on the right. If the use-by time has not come yet, the dough is not ready. No sooner.

The Mistake We Keep Making
Dough that proofs in the cooler does not proof. Cold dough fights the stretch, tears, and bakes dense. Room temperature, five hours, every time.
