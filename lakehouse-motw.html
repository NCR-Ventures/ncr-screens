Lake Erie Walleye
Crusted Walleye · Broccolini · Red Skin Potatoes · Lemon Butter Sauce
The Story

Two minutes, flesh side down. Season the fish, then dredge it in the Ritz cracker crust. Into a hot pan with oil, flesh side down, and leave it alone until the crust is golden brown. Then flip it and let the oven finish it. Move it early and the crust comes off in the pan.

Build it from the bottom up. Broccolini and the bay seasoned potatoes go down first, then three ounces of lemon butter, then the fish on top. Potatoes get finished in scampi butter in their own pan. Parsley last, right before it goes.

Walleye Capital Of The World
The Ohio Department of Natural Resources Division of Wildlife calls Lake Erie the Walleye Capital of the World. Hatches have run above average six years in a row, including the three largest ever surveyed. The menu says Lake Erie, so say Lake Erie.
