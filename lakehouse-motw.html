Shrimp & Crab Roll
Tarragon Mayo · Toasted Brioche · Lakeside Classic
The Story

Gulf shrimp and lump crab come together in a tarragon mayo — bright, herbal, just enough richness to hold everything together without weighing it down. Piled high on a toasted brioche roll, this is a summer sandwich done right.

Tarragon is the move here. Most rolls go mayo-forward and call it a day. This one uses the herb to do the heavy lifting — anise-forward, clean, and a little unexpected. The kind of detail that makes people ask what's in it.

Chef's Note
Tarragon is one of the four classic French fines herbes — alongside parsley, chervil, and chives. It's underused in American kitchens, which is exactly why it works so well here. Don't skip it.
