Short Rib “French Dip”
Caramelized onions · Swiss cheese · Au jus · Fries · Horseradish cream
The Story

Short rib instead of sliced roast beef. Braised until it pulls apart, piled on a toasted roll with caramelized onions and melted Swiss. The jus is the braising liquid, skimmed and seasoned, served hot on the side.

Sell the dip. Horseradish cream on the side, fries alongside. Tell the guest to dunk every bite. A French dip that goes out without hot jus is a mistake at the window, not the guest’s problem.

Where It Comes From
Los Angeles, not France. Cole’s claims it from 1908 and Philippe the Original from 1918, and the two have argued for a century. “French” is the roll, not the country.
