The Line Check
Twice a Day · Every Station · Before Every Service
Open and turn. Every station, every cooler, every hot well — before the doors open and again before the dinner push. It is not paperwork. It is the last chance to catch something before a guest does.
Eyes
Color, separation, ice crystals, dry edges. If it looks tired at 4:00 it will look worse at 8:00.
Nose
Smell it before you taste it. Your nose catches a turning product faster than your tongue will.
Taste
Clean spoon every time. Season, acid, consistency. Never double-dip into the pan.

Why It Holds Up
The best kitchens in the world run the same simple loop: look, smell, taste, log, initial. Build it so anyone can walk it, not just the chef — a check only one person can run is a check that stops when that person is off.
