Idaho Rainbow Trout
Beurre Blanc · Shrimp & Crab · Arugula & Tomato
The Story

Pan-seared Idaho rainbow trout under a beurre blanc with shrimp and crab, finished with chervil. Arugula and tomato salad, charred lemon.

Idaho rainbow trout sets the standard for U.S. farmed trout — mild, sweet, fresh, and built to carry a rich sauce without losing itself.

Beurre blanc is the classic French butter sauce. Loaded with shrimp and crab, it turns the trout into a composition. The arugula cuts the richness.

The Source
Idaho is the largest producer of farmed rainbow trout in the U.S. Spring-fed, cold-water raised, and prized for its mild, clean flavor.
