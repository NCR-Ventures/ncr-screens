Greek Salad
Tomato · Cucumber · Olive · Feta · Red Wine Vinaigrette
The Story

Vine-ripened tomatoes, cucumbers, olives, peppers, red onions, and feta. Dressed in red wine vinaigrette. Simple, bright, balanced.

Horiatiki — Greek "village salad" — is a study in restraint. No lettuce, no fillers. The quality of each ingredient is the whole dish.

Sweet tomato, cool cucumber, sharp onion, salty olive, creamy feta, peppery bite. The vinaigrette ties it together.
