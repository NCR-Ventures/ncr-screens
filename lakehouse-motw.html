The Story

Two kinds of heat coming at you from opposite directions. Calabrian chilies grow in the toe of Italy and bring a fruity, smoky burn — flavor first, not just fire. The hot honey hits sweet up front and the heat shows up a second later. That gap is the whole point of the dish.

Provolone brings the sharp, mozzarella brings the pull. The honey goes on after the bake, never before — it scorches in the oven and turns bitter. Keep the drizzle even across the whole round, or one slice is a different dish than the one beside it.

Sweet Heat · Brooklyn, 2010
Hot honey on pizza traces back to one guy. Mike Kurtz found jars of chili honey at a pizzeria in Brazil in 2003, spent years making his own, and brought it to Paulie Gee's in Brooklyn in 2010. It went on a spicy soppressata pie called the Hellboy, and the rest of the country followed.
