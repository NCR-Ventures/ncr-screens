Did You Know?

There is a dragonfly hiding in every one of our restaurants.

Prosperity
Across Asia the dragonfly is a sign of abundance and a good harvest. A full house every night. That is the idea.
Harmony
It lives on the line between water and air and moves between the two without a wasted motion. Sound like a good shift?
Good Luck
Sailors and fishermen called a dragonfly on the boat a lucky sign. We will take every bit of it we can get.

They were placed on purpose. Some are right out in the open. Some are tucked where you have walked past them a hundred times without looking up. Every building has one, Arlington included, and it was put there for a reason.

One more thing about dragonflies: they can fly backward, hover in place, and change direction mid-air. Every move is intentional. That is the standard on our floor and in our kitchens too.

Can you find them all?
Find yours first. Then see how many you can name across the company. No hints from the managers.
