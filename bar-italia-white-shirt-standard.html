FOH Appearance Standard

The White Shirt Standard

Bar Italia
Every Shift · Every Server
Collar Down, Buttoned
Crisp points, no curl, no fray. This is the look.
1
PRESSED
The dry cleaner is the standard. If you can’t iron it that sharp, take it to the cleaner. No wrinkles, no fold lines, no “it’ll relax.”
2
CLEAN
White means white. No stains, no collar ring, no pit marks. A white tee underneath keeps it sharp.
3
SHARP
Fully buttoned, tucked in, belt on. Sleeves down and buttoned or cleanly rolled — matching, not random.
4
BACKUP
A second pressed shirt in your locker. Spills happen mid-shift — you change, you don’t finish dirty.
◆
You are the first thing the guest sees. Walk the floor looking like you mean it.
