Team Benefit Guide ·
Un Vantaggio Del Lavoro · A Perk Of Working Here
Mangia Con Noi. It’s on the house — or pretty close to it.
Three ways the team eats at Bar Italia. The rules are short. The food is good. Read this once and you’re set.
I
Family Meal
Pasto in Famiglia
Before Service · On The House
Generally 10:30 AM and 4:00 PM at most locations, before doors open.
Could be eggs and hashbrowns. Could be spaghetti marinara. Whatever the kitchen made for the team that day.
It’s for you. No charge. No paperwork. Sit down and eat before your shift.
Not every store every day — ask your GM about your location’s schedule.
L’idea We feed each other before we feed the guests. Show up hungry.
II
Shift Meal
Pasto di Turno
On The Clock · 50% Off
Work a shift of 4.5 hours or more and you get one item at 50% off the menu.
Pay when you order. Don’t leave without paying — the next one is full price.
Not between 6–8 PM. That’s the rush. Eat before or after.
To-go after shift: one item. On a day off: two items. Manager rings it in.
Heads Up Food only. No kids’ menu, no happy hour items, no alcohol discount — 21 and up, 2-drink limit.
III
Dine With Us
Cena Con Noi
Off The Clock · Up To 50% Off
You alone: 50% off food. You + 1: 50%. You + 2: 30%. You + 3: 25%.
Visit any NCR location — every Bar Italia, 17 River, JoJo’s, Lola’s, Lakehouse, Beachwood, Arlington.
You need: 1 month employed, 3 shifts/week average, good standing.
Table only, off the clock. Not at the bar. Be a guest, not staff.
Fine Print Alcohol not discounted. 20% gratuity on the pre-discount total. You represent us — act like it.
!
The Reservation Rule
La Regola della Prenotazione
Read This Carefully
There is one way to book the dining discount. Just one.
1. You call your GM.
2. Your GM calls the GM where you’re dining.
3. That GM books it as an employee reservation.
No OpenTable. No Resy. No walking up to the host. No texting a friend who works there.
If You Skip This You pay full price. No retroactive discount — ever.
☎
GM To GM. By Phone. Every Time.
That call is the whole secret. Make it at least 24 hours ahead. Not Fri/Sat 5–9 PM. Not holidays. Patio seats need approval.
