Short Rib Pot Stickers
Red Wine Demi · Pan-seared · Braised
The Story

Short rib is braised low and slow in red wine demi until it falls apart, then folded into hand-crimped pot stickers and seared until the bottom crisps gold. The yuzu ponzu cuts through the richness — citrus and umami in one clean hit.

This one borrows from two traditions and commits to neither — and that's the point. It's a chef's technique dish: classical braise, Asian fold, bright acid finish. Fresh cilantro, shaved scallion, and a scatter of sesame close it out.

Chef's Note
Yuzu is a Japanese citrus — tarter than lemon, more aromatic than lime. Ponzu is the soy-citrus sauce built around it. Together they do what butter does to a pan sauce: they pull everything into focus.
