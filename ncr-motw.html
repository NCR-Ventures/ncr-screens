The Grille's Smash Burger
Double Smashed · American · House Sauce · Onion Straws
The Story

Two smashed beef patties, American cheese, lettuce, tomato, crispy onion straws on a toasted bun. CCG sauce in Beachwood, 17 sauce in Chagrin Falls — each kitchen's own.

Served with fries — made to order, hot from the fryer, seasoned in a copper bowl. Never pre-portioned, never sitting.

A mainstay at both Grilles since open. Different sauce, same standard — built fresh, smashed to order, never under a heat lamp.
