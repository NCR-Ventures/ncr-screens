Steak & Frites
Bistro Classic · Cooked to Temperature · Sauce Made In-House
The Story

Béarnaise is the whole dish. It is hollandaise with the lemon swapped out — egg yolk and butter emulsified over a reduction of white wine vinegar, shallot, and tarragon. It breaks if it gets too hot and it breaks if it sits. Make it close to service and hold it warm, not hot.

The steak goes out at the temperature that was ordered, and that is the one thing nobody can fix once the plate leaves the line. The arugula is not garnish — the pepper and the acid are there to cut the butter in the sauce. Fries hot and salted.

The Sauce · Saint-Germain-en-Laye, 1836
Chef Collinet built béarnaise for the opening night of Le Pavillon Henri IV outside Paris. He named it for Béarn — not because the sauce comes from there, but because that is where Henri IV was born.
