French Onion Soup
Provolone · Gruyère · Soup Crostini · Chives
The Story

Seventeen minutes in the steamer. The soup stays in the steamer the full seventeen minutes. Pour it into the crock, lay two crostini across the top, then provolone and gruyère centered over them. Off-center cheese leaves a bare spot, and that is where the crostini burns.

Browned, not burnt. Under the salamander it only needs to take color. Walk away and the gruyère turns bitter and the top goes black, and there is no saving it once it does. Chives go on after it comes out, never before.

Les Halles · Paris
French onion soup is associated with the market workers of Les Halles in nineteenth-century Paris, who ate it at odd hours around the city's main food market. Same handful of things we use: onions, stock, bread, and cheese. It lives or dies on the cheese browning right.
