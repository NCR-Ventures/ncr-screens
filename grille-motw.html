Idaho Rainbow Trout
Lobster butter sauce · Lobster meat · Mashed potatoes
The Story

Trout cooks fast. Respect that. The fillet goes skin-side down on a hot grill and comes off the second it flakes. Overcook it by a minute and it dries out. Lobster butter sauce goes over the top, then a spoon of lobster meat so the guest sees it before they taste it.

Two sides, two jobs. Mashed potatoes catch the sauce. Spicy broccolini with mascarpone butter cuts the richness. Plate it like the photo: fish front and center, sauce pooled, lobster on top, parsley last. A fish plate that eats like a steak plate. Sell it that way.

Where It Comes From
Most of America’s farm-raised rainbow trout comes from one stretch of southern Idaho, the Magic Valley, raised in cold spring water off the Snake River canyon. That water is why it tastes clean and mild. Say “Idaho,” not “the trout.”
