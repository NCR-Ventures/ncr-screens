Pre-Shift Agenda
Focus points of the week — some new, some repeated.
5:00 PM
Hard start time
Focus Points
1
Seat Numbers
Use them always. Ask for help when you need it.
2
The Greet
Waters on a tray. Fan out chargers if the host hasn't. Ask occasion, allergens, first time. Return timely.
3
Proper Coursing & Pre-Bussing
App plates off before entrées. Fresh glass for refills. Steak knives, pasta spoons, etc.
4
Missed Greets
If it's obvious, greet the table and get them waters. Then get a manager to decide who's taking it.
5
Know Your Wine
BTG wines behind the bar; bottle-only locked in the hallway. BTG list must be mastered — benchmark quizzes this week.
 
6
Double Increments
On a double / vol shift, run a report between shifts, clock out and in. Manager increments manually if needed.
7
Follow the Protein
Start with the protein, modify the set. Chix Marsala = Limone, sub Marsala set. Veal Parm = Veal, sub Parm set.
8
Get Clarity
Vague guest? Ask. Margarita — drink or pizza? Salmon — entrée or soft scramble? Red blend — Italian or Californian? Damilano — Barbera or Barolo?
9
Saturday Challenge
First person to host and hit all the points wins a case of High Noon (or equivalent prize).
