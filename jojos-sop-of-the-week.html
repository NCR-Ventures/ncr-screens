Station Reference Card
3–4 Min Steep
French Press Service
Step-by-step guide
for a perfect pour
01
Grind
Hold the press directly below the grinder bezel so no grounds spill outside.
02
Bloom
Fill a quarter of the way with hot water, then stir 3 times to saturate the grounds.
03
Fill & Cap
Pour water until just below the spout, then cap the press and let it steep.
04
Press & Serve
After 3–4 minutes, bring the press to the table. Press with the spout facing away from the guest to prevent spills.
05
Enjoy
Sit back and hear the compliments — you just served a perfect French press.
