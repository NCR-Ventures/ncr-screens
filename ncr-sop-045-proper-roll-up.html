The Proper Roll-Up
One roll-up, every table, every location. Polished silverware, clean pressed napkin, washed hands, clean surface. Then these seven steps.
THE RULE.
The first fold goes over the silverware. That fold is the only thing keeping the knife and fork in the napkin when the guest opens it. Skip it and the silverware ends up on the floor.
1Lay it flat
Diamond, point toward you. Hemmed side up.
2Fold to a triangle
Bottom point up to the top point. Long edge toward you.
3Place the silverware
Knife down, blade in. Fork on top. Centered, three fingers above the long edge.
4First Fold — over the silverware
Fold the long edge up and over so the silverware is covered. This fold is what keeps it from flying out when the guest opens it.
5Fold in one side
Take one point and fold it over the silverware. Keep the first fold flat.
6Press it
Press down along the silverware with the flat of your hand. Snug, not strangled.
7Roll it
Roll to the last point. Seam on the bottom.
THE CHECK.
Hold the roll by the middle and give it one light shake. Nothing moves. If the silverware slides or shows at either end, unroll it and start over at Step 3.
