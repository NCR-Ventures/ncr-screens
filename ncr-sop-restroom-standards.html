Restroom Standards
A clean restroom is not a bonus — it is how a guest grades the whole kitchen.
1
Checked every 20 minutes
Host — SA — Floor Manager. Logged every time.
2
Spotless — floors, fixtures, mirrors
Dry floors, clean bowls, streak-free mirrors, no odor on entry.
3
Fully stocked, always
Soap, paper towels, and toilet paper filled — spare roll on hand.
4
Waste bins never overflow
Liner intact, emptied before it is full — not after.
Why It Matters
The guest doesn't see the back of house — the line, the walk-in, the prep. But the restroom is how they judge how clean your kitchen is.
