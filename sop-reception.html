The Reception
FOH Standard
SOP-FOH-GREET-001
The Initial Greet
The First 90 Seconds.
Every guest decides — in the time it takes to say four words to you — whether they’re about to have a good night.
Hospitality · n.
The friendly and generous reception and entertainment of guests.
01
Smile First. Eyes Up.
Guest first. Reservation list second. Never a glance at the book before the smile.
02
Never “Do You Have a Reservation?”
It’s a wall. Say: “Welcome in — let me find you a great spot.”
03
Know Their Name.
Regulars greeted by name before they’ve said a word. That’s the game.
04
Every Guest Is a VIP.
Tuesday two-top = Saturday-night big table. Same smile. Same attention.
05
Own the First 90 Seconds.
For the guest, they are the only ninety seconds that matter.
You are the whole house, compressed into ninety seconds. Own it.
SOP-FOH-GREET-001
Effective 2026
